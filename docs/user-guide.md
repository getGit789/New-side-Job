# User guide

## For the agency owner and staff

**Roles.** The owner can do everything. Staff see only the projects they are assigned to. Clients see only their
own organization's projects, and only what is marked visible to them.

**Clients.** Clients → New client. Add contacts (the people who will log in). "Send portal invite" emails a single-use
link that works for 7 days. Contacts can be removed at any time; they lose access at once. Archive a client when all
its projects are closed. Import or export clients as CSV from the Clients page (owner).

**Projects.** Projects → New project. Assign staff (owner). The client fills in the brief from their side; you can ask
questions about it on the project page. Milestones with visibility "client" show on the client's page; "internal"
ones never do.

**Deliverables and versions.** A deliverable is one thing to approve (logo, homepage, contract). Add a version by
uploading a file or pasting a link. A version is a draft until you share it. Sharing notifies the client. A shared
version can be withdrawn but never edited or replaced. When the client asks for a revision, add the next version.
After an approval, adding another version needs a reason, and the approved version stays approved. The client
sees comments marked "visible to client"; internal notes stay internal.

**Invoices.** Record the invoice number, amount, due date, payment link and PDF. Mark it sent, then paid. Invoices
marked "client" show on the client's page once sent. Nothing is charged through BriefRelay.

**Hand-over and sign-off.** The project page shows which required deliverables are approved. The owner can waive
one with a reason. When every required deliverable is approved or waived, the client sees the "Sign off" button.
Sign-off records who, when, from which address, and exactly which versions, then closes the project. The owner can
reopen a closed project with a reason; the sign-off record stays.

**Team.** Owner only: invite staff, revoke invitations, remove staff. Activity shows the full audit log.

**Account.** Click your name in the top bar to change your password. It asks for the current password and logs out
other devices. "Forgot your password?" on the login page sends a one-hour, single-use link.

**Export and delete.** Owner only. A closed project can be exported as a zip: the client-visible record, decisions,
sign-off, audit trail and files. An archived client can be deleted permanently, with all its projects, files and
portal accounts. Export first; deletion cannot be undone.

## For clients

You get an email with an invitation link. Open it, choose a password, and you are in. You see only your own projects.

- **Brief.** Answer the questions and save as you go. Submit when done; your agency can then ask follow-up questions.
- **Review.** Open a deliverable to see the versions shared with you. Download the file or open the link. Leave a
  comment, then either **Approve** or **Request revision** with a note. Each decision is tied to one exact version, so
  there is never a question about which file you approved.
- **Invoices.** Sent invoices show with a download and a payment link. Payment happens on the link, outside this portal.
- **Sign off.** When everything required is approved, the sign-off button appears. Signing records the exact versions
  and closes the project. You can still read everything afterwards, but nothing can be changed.

Lost your password? Use "Forgot your password?" on the login page.
